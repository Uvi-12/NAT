What this artifact is for
-------------------------
This artifact demonstrates an authorization weakness (Approval-Provenance
Failure) in human-gated agentic, workflow, and CI/CD systems, and a defense
against it. Its intended use is scientific: to let reviewers reproduce the
paper's measurements and the defense evaluation, and to let practitioners test
their own resume path against the same predicate.

The security-relevant behavior it exercises is a write to a persisted
approval-relevant value followed by a normal resume. In this artifact that write
always targets a local, synthetic, in-process store that the harness itself
creates. The artifact is not an attack tool: it contacts no external service,
uses no credentials, and carries no exploit against any third-party deployment.

The provenance-bound verifier under artifact/mitigation is a reference
implementation and may be reused under the license in license.txt.
